The fly
-------
dendrite's itch is driven by a model of part of a real fruit fly's brain
(Drosophila melanogaster, an adult male): the neurons that answer music and
their connections, cut from Janelia's male CNS connectome and converted into
a compact table inside the plug-in. The neurons are simulated as in Shiu et
al. 2024, with learning, moods and the link to the echo added for dendrite.

Connectome data: Janelia Research Campus (FlyEM) and collaborators,
  male CNS v1.0. https://male-cns.janelia.org
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: the brain's neurons that fired on test material were selected
  (the nerve cord left out), their synapse counts weighted and re-encoded
  for real-time use, with the cell types, transmitters, sides and soma
  places needed to show them; nothing else of the data is distributed.

  Berg, S., Beckett, I. R., Costa, M., Schlegel, P. et al. Sexual dimorphism
    in the complete connectome of the Drosophila male central nervous
    system. Cell 189 (2026).

Brain model: Shiu, P. K. et al. A Drosophila computational brain model
  reveals sensorimotor processing. Nature 634, 210-219 (2024).
  Code: github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model (MIT licence).

The fly is a model inspired by these data. It is not a scientific
instrument, and Janelia and the authors above do not endorse dendrite.
