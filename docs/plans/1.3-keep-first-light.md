# dendrite 1.3: keep + first light (plan)

2026-11-12. Hakan approved the creature plan and gave full control. This thread builds the two layers
that come before the creature; the creature thread builds the mind (`Source/dsp/Itch.*`,
`Source/dsp/Creature.*`) and merges both into one build. Neither layer changes a sample of the sound.

The main goal of the project stays the same: the itch should feel alive and think on its own.
Keep is how a living, never-repeating itch stays usable: you can't freeze a creature, but you can
keep what it did. Keep's diary of events is the creature's first memory.

## keep: the last minute, always

**What it holds.** Two tapes, always recording, about 60 s each (at most 5.76 M frames, so 30 s at 192 kHz):
everything dendrite plays, and the itch alone (the heads, no dry, no echo). A diary of marks: every
itch (start, end of its tail, which heads, which length), every throw and every runaway, each with
its sample, song position, tempo and bar length. Mark format is the one the creature uses:
`{sample, ppq, bpm, kind, heads mask, frame choice}`.

**What you see.**
- After an itch its three lamps stay as **embers**, dimming as the minute forgets it.
- Hover the strip and **the tape** appears along its top edge: the last minute, left (a minute ago) to right (now),
  with a red stroke where each itch played, grey where you threw, white where it ran away.

**What you do.**
- **Drag an ember lamp** into Live: the last itch alone, from the bar line before it to the bar line after its tail.
- **Drag a stroke** on the tape: that itch (or throw, or runaway, with everything that played).
- **Press anywhere else on the tape and pull**: everything from that bar to the last full bar.
- While you press, a **ruler** shows what you are taking: its bars, a 1 2 3 4 beat grid, its heads and its length.

**Files.** `Documents/dendrite/kept/`, never a temp folder, never deleted. 32-bit float WAV with the tempo,
beats and metre written into the ACID chunk, and a name that says what it is:
`dendrite itch · scream laugh ghost · 2 bars · 124 bpm · 21-43-07.wav`. The same mark dragged twice reuses its file.

**Live on Windows.** Live can crash when a plug-in starts a drag while another window has focus
(JUCE forum 65028): dendrite hands Live's window focus right before the drag.

## first light: dendrite shows itself

The first time dendrite opens on a computer (and again when a version adds a lesson), the window plays a
silent ~22 s demonstration with a ghost hand, one deadpan line per step:

1. *this is the light. drag it.* The ghost drags the light; up is more echo, right is more space.
2. *touch the dark. it throws.* The ghost presses the dark; the nerve fires, the light flares.
3. *when the music repeats itself, it itches.* The itch LED warms, three heads fire in turn, their names light.
4. *it keeps the last minute. pull an itch into your song.* The ghost pulls an ember lamp up and out.
5. The wordmark glows: *click the name to see this again.*

It never touches the sound or a parameter (the ghost moves a puppet of the light). Any click ends it.
A small settings file per computer remembers it has run. Clicking the wordmark replays it.
