v1.2  ·  vst3 · au  ·  windows · macos

let's look at a sound. there. not there. give it two tape heads, a dotted ⅛ and a ¼, and it comes back, each time a little further out: inside your head, then around it. play the same thing too long and it gets an itch.

start

put it on a track. no send. no mix knob.

play. the default already works.

touch the dark on the last hit of a phrase.

now move the light while it plays.

1light

this point is the whole interface. drag it.

up, the repeats live longer. whisper · dub · drown · endless

right, they live further away. inside · around · envelop · beyond

every hit grows a dendrite. young stems follow the light, old ones peel off and die. yours do the same, only smaller.

2throw

touch the dark and hold. a nerve runs from your finger to the light, and what is playing is thrown into the echo, louder, off to one side.

let go. it falls home over two bars.

at endless, hold on. it runs away. let go before it eats the room.

light at the bottom: only what you throw gets in. dub engineers call it a closed send.

3itch

how long it can stand the same thing. drag the dots: ¼ bar to 8 bars, or off.

when bars start to look alike, it takes the loudest hit. three of five heads answer:

scream
rises and circles you
laugh
ha ha ha, side to side, falling
manipulate
a hand on the reel
swarm
a dozen grains fall into step
ghost
a whisper behind you

then quiet. on headphones you can point at each one. red is only ever the itch.

three places to begin

dub 50 · around 30a chord stab. throw the last one.

drown 70 · envelop 65a voice, on headphones. itch off.

whisper 25 · around 40a loop. itch 4. leave it be.

beneath

each pass loses top and low mids, gains grit, and wobbles with a wow that never repeats.

space only moves the sides, so mono stays a clean dub echo. a scandalous simplification, but true.

notes

no latency. your track passes untouched. the echo never outgrows it, unless you hold on at endless.

time: dotted, triplet, half. dry off on a return track.

map throw to a key: ctrl+k in live.

shift-drag for fine moves. double-click the light: home. the wheel moves it too.
