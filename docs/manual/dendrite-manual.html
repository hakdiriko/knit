a dub echo. dotted, in time, safe in mono. each repeat steps a little further out: inside your head, then all around it. repeat yourself too long, and it itches.

start

put it straight on a track. no send, no mix knob.

play. the default already works.

throw the last hit of a phrase.

move the light while it plays.

1light

drag it.

up is how long the repeats live. whisper · dub · drown · endless

right is where they live. inside · around · envelop · beyond

every hit grows a branch.

2throw

hold. what is playing falls into the echo, louder, off to one side.

let go. it drifts home over two bars.

at the bottom of the light, only what you throw gets in.

3itch

how long it can stand the same thing: ¼ bar to 8 bars, or off.

it listens, grows restless, takes the loudest hit. three of five heads answer:

scream
rises and circles you
laugh
ha ha ha, side to side, falling
manipulate
a hand drags the reel
swarm
grains that fall into step
ghost
a whisper behind your head

then quiet. headphones show it best.

three places to begin

dub 50 · around 30a chord stab. throw the last one.

drown 70 · envelop 65a voice, on headphones. itch off.

whisper 25 · around 40a loop. itch 4. leave it be.

beneath

two tape heads, the dotted ⅛ and the ¼, in one loop. each pass: less top, thinner lows, a little grit, a wow that never repeats.

space only moves the sides. fold to mono and a clean dub echo stays.

notes

no latency. never louder than what you played into it. bypass fades in 30 ms.

map throw to a key: ctrl+k in live.

shift-drag for fine moves. double-click goes home.
